ProxAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

ProxAI is a personal assistant used only by its owner. It is not offered to anyone else. This page explains what it does with Google user data.

What it accesses

  • Calendars it creates (calendar.app.created): it creates one calendar named "ProxAI" and can add, move and delete events on that calendar only.
  • Your events, read-only (calendar.events.readonly): it reads the title, time and location of events on calendars the owner chooses, to answer questions such as "what's on my calendar tomorrow". It cannot change or delete these events.
  • Free/busy times (calendar.freebusy): when the owner is busy, to avoid double-booking.
  • The owner's business mailbox, read-only (gmail.readonly), for one separate business address only (not the owner's personal email): it reads new messages so replies from businesses the owner contacted are filed with that contact, and so "unsubscribe" or "no thanks" replies stop further email to that business.
  • Sending from that mailbox (gmail.send): it sends an email only after the owner has read that exact message and approved it.
  • Files it creates in Google Drive (drive.file): one folder named "ProxAI backups" and the nightly backup files it puts there, which are encrypted on the owner's computer before upload. With this access it can't see or open any other file in the owner's Drive.

It cannot delete, archive, label or change any email. It does not access the owner's personal Gmail, any other Drive files, Contacts or any other Google service, and it never sends calendar invitations.

How the data is used and stored

Calendar data is fetched on request and used only to answer the owner. Business email is checked every few minutes; each message is filed with the matching business contact on the owner's own computer. The sign-in tokens are stored on that computer, readable only by the owner's account. Changes ProxAI makes to its own calendar, and the emails it sends, are logged locally there. Each night, an encrypted copy of the owner's business records goes to the "ProxAI backups" folder and is read back once to check it. Only the owner holds the passphrase that opens it, and the copies are never sent to the AI model provider.

To answer a question or draft a reply for the owner to approve, the relevant calendar details or email text may be sent to the AI model provider that powers the assistant, as part of that one task. Google user data is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising or to train AI models.

ProxAI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove ProxAI's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (in each account it was given), delete the "ProxAI" calendar in Google Calendar, and delete the "ProxAI backups" folder in Google Drive.
