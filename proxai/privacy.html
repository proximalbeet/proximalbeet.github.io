ProxAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

ProxAI is a personal assistant used only by its owner. It is not offered to anyone else. This page explains what it does with Google user data.

What it accesses

  • Calendars it creates (calendar.app.created): it creates one calendar named "ProxAI" and can add, move and delete events on that calendar only.
  • Your events, read-only (calendar.events.readonly): it reads the title, time and location of events on calendars the owner chooses, to answer questions such as "what's on my calendar tomorrow". It cannot change or delete these events.
  • Free/busy times (calendar.freebusy): when the owner is busy, to avoid double-booking.

It does not access Gmail, Drive, Contacts or any other Google service, and it never sends invitations.

How the data is used and stored

Calendar data is fetched on request and used only to answer the owner. The sign-in token is stored on the owner's own computer, readable only by the owner's account. Changes ProxAI makes to its own calendar are logged locally on that computer.

To answer a question, the relevant calendar details may be sent to the AI model provider that powers the assistant, as part of that one conversation. Google user data is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising or to train AI models.

Removing access

The owner can remove ProxAI's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the "ProxAI" calendar in Google Calendar.
