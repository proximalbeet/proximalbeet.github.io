ProxAI

A personal assistant that runs on one person's own computer.

ProxAI is a private, single-user assistant built and used by its owner (GitHub: proximalbeet). It is not a public service and doesn't accept other users.

It connects to its owner's Google Calendar so it can answer "what's on my calendar" and add events to a separate "ProxAI" calendar. See the privacy policy for exactly what it can access.
