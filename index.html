The rules every agent follows

An agent talks to your customers, so it works like your best office person: polite, honest and careful.

It says it's an AI. Every customer knows they're talking to your AI assistant, from the first message.
It sticks to your facts. Only the services, prices and answers you approve. No made-up promises or times.
It hands off to you. Anything it can't answer becomes a message for you, with the customer's details.
Your data stays yours. Each business gets its own private setup. Nothing is shared, sold or used to train AI.
