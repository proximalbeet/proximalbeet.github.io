How it works

No software to learn on your own, and nothing goes live before you've seen it work.

  1. A free 20-minute lookWe go over how you answer calls, quote and follow up today, and where the time goes. Then you get a fixed price in writing.
  2. Built from your facts, tested firstYour services, prices, area and rules go in. The agent is tested with made-up customers and security-checked before a real customer ever talks to it.
  3. Live, and looked afterIt goes on your website, and I keep an eye on it. Half up front on jobs over $500, the rest when it's done.

Our rules for every agent

An agent talks to your customers, so every agent I put in front of them is held to these, like your best office person: polite, honest and careful.

It says it's an AI. Every customer knows they're talking to your AI assistant, from the first message.
It sticks to your facts. Only the services, prices and answers you approve. No made-up promises or times.
It hands off to you. Anything it can't answer goes to you as a message, with the customer's details.
Your data stays yours. Each business gets its own private agent, with its own server, keys and storage. Nothing is shared, sold or used to train AI.
