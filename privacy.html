Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

ProxAI is Nathan Kenney's small business in Michigan. It builds practical tools for trades businesses, and it runs a private assistant, also called ProxAI, that helps with that work. This page says what information ProxAI collects, why, and what it never does.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no trackers and no forms. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's privacy statement.

When you contact ProxAI

If you email, ProxAI keeps your message, your name, your contact details and what you need, to answer you and do the work.

Phone calls and text messages

  • Calls to the ProxAI line are answered by an AI assistant or go to voicemail. It says it's an AI and that the call is recorded and transcribed, first thing. The transcript, a short summary and your number are kept with your message so Nathan can call you back. Recordings are kept on Nathan's computer for 30 days, then deleted; the transcript stays with your message.
  • Who ProxAI calls or texts: only people who called or texted the ProxAI line first, about what they asked, or who agreed to calls or texts. ProxAI never makes cold AI calls or sends cold texts, and Nathan approves every outgoing text. Calls and texts are sent only between 9 am and 7 pm your time.
  • Texts: when you agree to texts (for example on a call, or by texting the line), ProxAI may text you about your request: replies, callbacks and appointment confirmations, usually 1 to 4 messages per request. Message and data rates may apply. Every text says it's from ProxAI's AI assistant.
  • Stop at any time: reply STOP to any text, or say "don't call" on a call or voicemail, and ProxAI stops all contact with you, by phone, text and email, usually within minutes. Reply HELP for help, or email hello@getproxai.com.
  • Your phone number isn't shared: no mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-message opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party.
  • Calls and texts run through Twilio, and the assistant's voice is made with ElevenLabs. They handle the call or message only to deliver it.

Businesses ProxAI contacts

  • To offer a service, ProxAI may send one short email to a business, using contact details the business makes public (its website, or business listings such as Overture Maps).
  • Every email says who it's from and how to stop. Reply "no thanks", "unsubscribe" or "stop", and the business is marked do-not-contact for every service, usually within minutes. That record is kept so it stays that way.
  • ProxAI never buys email lists, and never offers the same business the same service twice.

Clients

  • For clients, ProxAI keeps what's needed to build and run what they asked for: contact details, the agreement, invoices and payments, and the work itself.
  • Client systems run in the client's own accounts, or in a separate cloud project for each client. Client data is kept on Nathan's own computer only as long as the work needs it.
  • Client data is never used for anyone else's work or for advertising.

AI, and the ProxAI assistant

  • ProxAI uses AI models (Anthropic's Claude) to help research and to draft emails and replies. The text a task needs, such as the email being answered, is sent to the AI provider for that task.
  • Nathan reads and approves every email before it's sent. Nothing goes out on its own.
  • ProxAI doesn't sell or rent information, and doesn't use it to train AI models.

Google user data

The ProxAI assistant uses Google sign-ins that only its owner gives it:

  • Calendars it creates (calendar.app.created): one calendar named "ProxAI", where it adds, moves and deletes its own events.
  • The owner's events, read-only (calendar.events.readonly) and free/busy times (calendar.freebusy): to answer "what's on my calendar" and avoid double-booking. It can't change or delete these events.
  • The business mailbox (gmail.readonly and gmail.send), for the business addresses only, never the owner's personal email: it reads new messages so a reply is filed with its contact and a "no thanks" stops further email, and it sends an email only after the owner approves that exact message. It can't delete, archive, label or change any email.
  • Files it creates in Google Drive (drive.file): one "ProxAI backups" folder with nightly backups, encrypted on the owner's computer before upload. It can't see any other file.

Google user data isn't sold, shared for advertising, or used to train AI models. ProxAI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The owner can remove this access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Who else sees it

Only the services the work needs: Google Workspace (email), Twilio (calls and texts), ElevenLabs (the assistant's voice), the AI provider (drafts and the phone assistant), a client system's hosting provider, and the bank or payment app for payments. Nothing is sold.

How long it's kept

  • Businesses contacted: as long as needed to never contact them twice and to honor opt-outs. Ask, and everything except the do-not-contact record is deleted.
  • Calls and texts: recordings 30 days; transcripts, summaries and texts with your message, as long as it's an open request, and deleted on request. Consent and opt-out records are kept so your choice holds.
  • Clients: as long as the work and tax records require.
  • Backups: encrypted nightly copies in Google Drive that only Nathan can open.

Your choices

Email hello@getproxai.com to see what ProxAI keeps about you or your business, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Reply "no thanks" to any ProxAI email, or STOP to any text, to stop all contact.
